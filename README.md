# Retail Sales & Profit Analysis
## Project Overview
This project analyzes retail sales data using Excel, SQL, Python, and Power BI to understand sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, and regional trends.

## Key Business Insights
### 1. Overall Performance
* Total Sales: ₹7,489,843
* Total Profit: ₹946,464
* The business generated positive overall profit during the analyzed period.

### 2. Category Performance
* Technology, Furniture, and Office Supplies were compared based on total sales and profit.
* Category-level analysis helps identify which product categories contribute more to revenue and profitability.

### 3. Regional Performance
* Sales and profit were analyzed across different regions.
* This helps identify regions with stronger sales performance and areas that may require further attention.

### 4. Monthly Performance
* Monthly sales and profit trends were analyzed using Python and Power BI.
* The trends help identify periods of higher and lower business activity.

### 5. Product Performance
* The Top 10 products by sales were identified.
* Loss-making products were also analyzed to identify products that generate negative profit.

### 6. Customer Performance
* Customers were analyzed based on their total sales and profit contribution.
* High-value and loss-making customers can be identified from the analysis.

### 7. Discount and Profit
* The relationship between discount and profit was analyzed using Python.
* This helps understand whether higher discounts are associated with changes in profitability.

## Tools Used
* Excel — Data cleaning and preparation
* SQL — Data querying and business analysis
* Python — Data analysis and visualization
* Power BI — Interactive dashboard and reporting

## Conclusion
The project demonstrates an end-to-end data analysis workflow, starting from raw data cleaning and continuing through SQL analysis, Python-based analysis, and Power BI dashboard development.
